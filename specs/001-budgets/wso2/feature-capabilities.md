| Overlapping budgets / override | **Most specific wins.** Budget B overrides budget A for a request when both match, they share context, period and owner dimensions, and B is narrower (a values list where A has "each", or a proper subset of A's values — e.g. provider ∈ {OpenAI} $500 overrides provider ∈ {OpenAI, Anthropic} $2,000 for OpenAI traffic): project-a `sub = alice` $50 replaces project-a `sub = each` $10 for alice, whose spend is then not charged to the $10 budget.
